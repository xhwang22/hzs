\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We study agents as data researchers that iteratively construct executable
environments for model training. \method{} organizes this process around a
traceable behavioral map and target-conditioned context refinement, supported
by iterative environment construction. Our analyses identify gaps in behavioral
understanding, feedback linkage, and environment implementation. Preliminary
training results show useful checkpoints but also regressions, motivating
controlled studies of sustained improvement. Further discussion and
limitations appear in Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}.
