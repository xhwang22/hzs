\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Data-centric RSI.}\quad
RSIBench-Data studies agents that revise training-data strategies for a
separate target model under fixed training, evaluation, and resource
controls~\citep{rsibenchdata}. Autodata develops an agentic data scientist
that iterates between data creation and quality assessment, including
meta-optimization of the data scientist itself~\citep{autodata}. DataMaster
combines data discovery and transformation with tree search and cumulative
memory while holding the learning algorithm fixed~\citep{datamaster}.
\method{} studies this research role for executable training environments:
a researcher starts with an empty environment library and uses
behavioral evidence to guide successive rounds of environment revision and
online RL. Its focus is the evidence supporting those research decisions.

\textbf{Executable environment synthesis for tool-calling agents.}\quad
AgentScaler, EnvScaler, and ScaleEnv automate the construction of executable
tools, state, and verifiable tasks~\citep{agentscaler,envscaler,scaleenv}.
Agent World Model scales database-backed synthetic environments for RL,
while EnvFactory combines executable environment construction with
topology-aware task synthesis~\citep{awm,envfactory}. FACET grounds task
instructions, solutions, and verifiers in shared executable state to preserve
source intent and cross-artifact consistency~\citep{facet}. The ACE framework
and WRIT further examine data accuracy, learner-relative complexity,
diversity, and the evidence required for individual tool-use
decisions~\citep{ace,writ}. These works establish construction and validation
principles that \method{} builds on; our emphasis is using source-traceable
behavioral analysis and actual-training feedback to decide which mechanisms
to construct or revise.

\textbf{Iterative RL and environment adaptation.}\quad
PAIRED and Prioritized Level Replay adapt environment generation or selection
using regret and learning-potential signals~\citep{paired,plr}. For tool-use
agents, RODS uses online reward statistics to synthesize structurally aligned
tasks and update a replay pool~\citep{rods}. EnvHarness adapts existing
environments from policy trajectories while retaining their original
verifiers~\citep{envharness}. SPADE trains a shared environment-designer and
solver model through self-play, using hint-based regret, corpus grounding,
and environment memory~\citep{spade}. Agent-World and AgentOmnia also use
behavioral diagnosis to guide targeted task synthesis and
self-evolution~\citep{agentworld,agentomnia}. \method{} uses an external
researcher to revise independent environments without the target backend or
verifier. It connects the source evidence behind a diagnosis to realized
program versions, training tasks, RL observations, and fixed-target
evaluation, supporting inspection of what each intervention actually delivered.
