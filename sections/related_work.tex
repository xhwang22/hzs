\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Data-centric research for RSI.}
RSIBench-Data evaluates a frontier researcher that revises training-data strategies for a separate target model under shared service and resource controls~\citep{rsibenchdata}. It supplies task-matched public seed repositories or examples; it does not prescribe a particular synthesis algorithm. Each candidate in its main setting uses LoRA SFT from a fixed base. \method{} studies a concrete executable-environment strategy, starting from an empty task-environment bank and continuing the student's online RL state. Both address the cross-model data-research component of RSI, not a claim that the student takes over or retrains the researcher. Autodata and DataMaster are further precedents for autonomous, downstream-feedback-driven data engineering~\citep{autodata,datamaster}.

\paragraph{Executable environment synthesis.}
AgentScaler, EnvScaler, ScaleEnv, Agent World Model, and EnvFactory establish programmatic tools, synthetic state, task generation, and generated verification as training infrastructure~\citep{agentscaler,envscaler,scaleenv,awm,envfactory}. FACET grounds task artifacts in a shared realized environment~\citep{facet}. We likewise bind requests and verification to concrete task data. Our emphasis is the persistent research process: complete environment programs retain identities and revisions, can be reused without code changes, and are reconsidered using target behavior and subsequent training feedback. Neither a database nor reusable task generation is claimed as a standalone invention.

\paragraph{Environment adaptation and self-play.}
EnvHarness reshapes an existing executable environment through Stage, Contract, and Chain components while retaining the original verifier; EnvRigger diagnoses policy behavior to author adaptations~\citep{envharness}. Our researcher does not receive that target implementation and instead constructs independent training surrogates and their verifiers. This removes a reuse assumption but incurs additional modeling and verification risk. SPADE also generates complete stateful environments, including backend tool state, later user instructions, and state-based checks~\citep{spade}. Its designer and solver share a trained model, with corpus grounding, environment memory, and hint-based regret. \method{} uses a fixed external Planner, target-development traces, and explicit library-management decisions; the studied runs use no external grounding corpus. These are different information and optimization settings, not evidence of superiority over SPADE's reported scores.

\paragraph{Curriculum and targeted synthesis.}
Unsupervised environment design and replay prioritize learning opportunities using regret or related signals~\citep{paired,plr}. RODS uses online reward statistics to generate skill-aligned variants while preserving API dependency structure and managing a replay pool~\citep{rods}. Agent-World and AgentOmnia also adapt task coverage to diagnosed weaknesses~\citep{agentworld,agentomnia}. \method{} additionally permits program-level changes to interaction and outcome semantics, while preserving interpretable target-task associations. Its reward-group statistics come from formal training, not an additional student admission test or a new gradient estimator. The Accuracy--Complexity--diversity framework and WRIT help explain why correctness, coverage, and relevant decision burden should be considered jointly~\citep{ace,writ}.
