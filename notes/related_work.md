# Related-work map

Primary PDFs in `OpenAgentScaler/paper/pdfs/` were extracted to `sources/paper_text/` (36 papers). The closest papers' introductions and method sections were read against the maintained implementation. Bibliographic metadata for the cited arXiv papers was checked against their primary abstract records on 2026-09-09; BFCL was checked against its PMLR proceedings entry. Existing repository reports were used as navigation and historical interpretation, not treated as substitutes for primary papers.

## Distinctions that need experimental support

The strongest candidate contribution is a specific method within *data-centric research for RSI*: a World--Scene--Task hierarchy as the editable experience source, persistent executable Scene constructor/verifier pairs, and program/mixture revision coupled to continued student learning. Coverage and separate local/probe evidence support that loop. Establish utility through fixed-bank and parameter-only controls, hierarchy/verifier-binding audits, and budget-matched iteration reliability. RSIBench-Data supplies the closest problem framing; EnvHarness remains a close architectural comparison. A matrix of unsupported checkmarks would exaggerate distinctions; the prose above records concrete scopes instead.
